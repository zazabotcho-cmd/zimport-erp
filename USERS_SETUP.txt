USERS — CREATE NEW USER

Open Settings > Users > Create New User.
Enter the full name, username, email, password, role and permitted pages.
Choose Send invitation to email to send a secure invitation link.
Passwords are not sent by email or stored in program data/backups.
The account can sign in with its email or username.
Use Edit / Page Access to change pages or role, reset passwords, resend a sign-in invitation, and activate/deactivate users.
Administrators always have full access. Users, currency/program settings and Head recycling administration are administrator-only.
Existing accounts retain their existing access until an administrator assigns an explicit page list.
Pages share underlying supplier, item, tender and order records; their dependencies are allowed together at the database level.
