RECYCLING BIN UPDATE

1. RECYCLING BIN is now a top-level main navigation page and is accessible to all signed-in users.
2. Deleted business records are routed to the Recycling Bin instead of being permanently removed.
3. Restore is available to every user role from the Recycling Bin.
4. Delete Permanently is displayed and permitted only for Administrator users.
5. Delete Expired Now and Retention Period controls are Administrator-only.
6. Permanent-delete functions include an additional administrator role check in code.
7. Existing ordinary delete permissions remain unchanged; moving records into the Recycling Bin does not grant extra delete rights.
8. Covered record areas include submissions, archived submissions, tenders, tender items, suppliers and supplier items, POs, transportation, financial/supplier/agency/other invoices, archived invoices, archived transportation, archived POs, LCBO submission work items, and country sourcing records.
