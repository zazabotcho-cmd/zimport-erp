PURCHASED / OFFICE-WAREHOUSE STOCK SPLIT (2026-11-24)

In Stock Transportation
- Action "Move to Office/Warehouse Stock" is renamed "Purchased".
- Clicking it opens a box asking for two quantities:
  Cases for Non-Licensed Customers and Cases for Licensed Customers.
  The total is taken from LCBO Trillium stock and added to Office/Warehouse Stock
  in the matching customer group.
- The Office/Warehouse column on this page also shows the Non-Licensed / Licensed split.

Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory
- "Cases in Office / Cases in Storage" is removed.
- New column "Office/Warehouse Stock" shows, on one row, the Non-Licensed and Licensed
  case quantities (editable to move cases between the two groups; total stays the same).
- "Sold to Non-Licensed Customer" takes cases only from the Non-Licensed stock,
  "Sold to Licensed Customer" only from the Licensed stock. Returns go back to the
  group they were sold from.
- "+ Add Inventory" now asks Non-Licensed and Licensed case quantities.
- New summary cards: Stock for Non-Licensed Customers / Stock for Licensed Customers.

Existing office stock (moved before this update) starts as Non-Licensed;
change the Licensed number on its row if part of it is for licensed customers.

Files changed: index.html only.

UPDATE (same day)
- In Stock Transportation, ACTION column: "Sold to the Non-licensed customer",
  "Sold to the Licensed customer" and "Archive" buttons removed. Only "Purchased"
  and "Return" remain.
- In Stock Transportation: "Inventory in stock office/warehouse" column removed
  (office stock is managed on the Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory page).

UPDATE 2 (same day)
- In Stock Transportation: "Other Fees per Bottle" column (with Total Lab Cost) now sits
  right after ACTION, where the office/warehouse column used to be.
- New column next to it: "Prime Cost per Bottle (TAX/HST incl.)", calculated automatically
  for Non-licensed and Licensed customers:
  proforma net bottle price + TAX/HST + other fees + transportation + lab cost per bottle.
  Profit per Bottle = Final Retail Price - Prime Cost (same result as before).

UPDATE 3 (same day)
- Column order is now: ... | Other Fees per Bottle | ACTION | Prime Cost per Bottle | If Final Retail Price ... 

UPDATE 4 (same day)
- In Stock Transportation: "LCBO Item #" column removed. The item # still shows under the Product Name.

UPDATE 5 (same day)
- "Prime Cost per Bottle (TAX/HST incl.)" moved from In Stock Transportation to the
  Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory page (after Office/Warehouse Stock). Same automatic
  calculation, using the item's PO pricing, transportation cost and lab cost.

UPDATE 6 (same day)
- Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory: one Final Retail Price per Bottle and one Item total
  retail value per item (no separate Non-Licensed/Licensed prices). Summary shows one
  Stock Total Retail Value. "+ Add Inventory" asks for one retail price.

UPDATE 7 (same day)
- Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory: "Sold to Non-Licensed Customers" and "Sold to Licensed
  Customers" columns replaced by one "Sold to" column with three: Restaurant/Hotels,
  Online Store, In Store. Each button asks Cases and Bottles sold.
  Restaurant/Hotels takes Licensed stock; Online Store and In Store take Non-Licensed stock.
- Stock now shows part cases as "X cases + Y bottles".
- Return button asks which of the three it was sold to, plus cases and bottles.
- Summary cards: Sold to Restaurant/Hotels / Online Store / In Store.

UPDATE 8 (2026-11-25)
- In Stock Transportation: "Total cases sold / Cases sold per month" and
  "Total profit / Monthly profit" columns removed.
- In Stock Transportation / All In Stock Transportations: statistics now in two rows of squares.
  Row 1 "Project": Total Project Budget, Total Project Cases/Bottles, Total Project Retail Value
  (Non-Licensed/Licensed), Proforma Project Balance, Transportation/Lab Cost/Other Fees,
  Sell-Out Months & Project Profit.
  Row 2 "Live": POs in Inventory, Cases/Bottles in Stock, Live Retail Value (Non-Licensed/Licensed),
  Proforma Balance Owing (+ Office/Warehouse stock and Approximate Stock Profit on the All page only).
- "If Final Retail Price per Bottle" (In Stock Transportation) and "Final Retail Price per Bottle"
  (Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory) are no longer connected. Existing office items keep the price
  they showed before; items moved to Office/Warehouse from now on start with an empty retail price.
- Statistics: "Project POs in Inventory" added under Total Project Cases / Bottles (all POs in the project).
  "POs in Inventory" moved under Total Cases / Bottles in Stock (POs that still have cases in stock).
- Total Project Budget square now shows its calculation in light grey text:
  Proforma Project Balance + Transportation + Lab Cost + Other Fees = Total Project Budget.
- Total Project Budget: Other Fees now = Other Fees per Bottle x bottles of each PO (was counted once per PO).
  Budget = Proforma Case Price + TAX/HST x cases + Transportation + Total Lab Cost (per PO) + Other Fees.
- Statistics: Live squares now sit directly under the matching Project squares
  (Cases/Bottles & POs, Retail Value, Proforma Balance).
- Total Project Budget title is now "(CAD, supplier paid from case price)".
- New square (Live row, under Total Project Budget): Project Sales Revenue (TAX/HST incl.) and
  Project Net Profit = Sales Revenue - Total Project Budget, with grey breakdown.
  Sales Revenue = bottles sold x selling price (Office/Warehouse sales use the Office/Warehouse
  retail price; other sales use the In Stock Transportation retail price).
