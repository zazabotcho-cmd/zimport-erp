PURCHASED / OFFICE-WAREHOUSE STOCK SPLIT (2026-11-24)

In Stock Transportation
- Action "Move to Office/Warehouse Stock" is renamed "Purchased".
- Clicking it opens a box asking for two quantities:
  Cases for Non-Licensed Customers and Cases for Licensed Customers.
  The total is taken from LCBO Trillium stock and added to Office/Warehouse Stock
  in the matching customer group.
- The Office/Warehouse column on this page also shows the Non-Licensed / Licensed split.

Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory
- "Cases in Office / Cases in Storage" is removed.
- New column "Office/Warehouse Stock" shows, on one row, the Non-Licensed and Licensed
  case quantities (editable to move cases between the two groups; total stays the same).
- "Sold to Non-Licensed Customer" takes cases only from the Non-Licensed stock,
  "Sold to Licensed Customer" only from the Licensed stock. Returns go back to the
  group they were sold from.
- "+ Add Inventory" now asks Non-Licensed and Licensed case quantities.
- New summary cards: Stock for Non-Licensed Customers / Stock for Licensed Customers.

Existing office stock (moved before this update) starts as Non-Licensed;
change the Licensed number on its row if part of it is for licensed customers.

Files changed: index.html only.

UPDATE (same day)
- In Stock Transportation, ACTION column: "Sold to the Non-licensed customer",
  "Sold to the Licensed customer" and "Archive" buttons removed. Only "Purchased"
  and "Return" remain.
- In Stock Transportation: "Inventory in stock office/warehouse" column removed
  (office stock is managed on the Office/Warehouse Stock Inventory page).

UPDATE 2 (same day)
- In Stock Transportation: "Other Fees per Bottle" column (with Total Lab Cost) now sits
  right after ACTION, where the office/warehouse column used to be.
- New column next to it: "Prime Cost per Bottle (TAX/HST incl.)", calculated automatically
  for Non-licensed and Licensed customers:
  proforma net bottle price + TAX/HST + other fees + transportation + lab cost per bottle.
  Profit per Bottle = Final Retail Price - Prime Cost (same result as before).
